## Recommended S1b contract to settle next

1. One seller per order, physical merchandise, GHS and collection only. Active self membership plus verified email for member-only items; public products do not require dues or Community. Snapshot eligibility at quote creation. A quoted price survives membership changes only for its bounded acceptance window; access to private catalog data still revalidates.
2. Ten-minute server quote and stock allocation, using the existing `commerce.inventory:<variant UUID>` lock. No client prices or free-form stock deduction. Stock corrections cannot take physical stock below active allocations. Payment success consumes once; expiry releases once; late captures enter reconciliation, never an oversale.
3. Require an explicit store commercial agreement before checkout: superadmin-owned percentage/cap/bearer, including explicit zero waiver, and supported settlement mode. Do not borrow the historical 10%, change existing gateway configuration, or infer merchandise fees from an Event. Recommend platform collection first only if the owner explicitly accepts seller-payable, payout, refund and support ownership; otherwise keep checkout disabled until own-gateway reconciliation is specified.
4. Separate payment attempts, immutable quote/terms snapshots, order state and fulfilment state. One verifier/confirmer validates provider account, reference, metadata, currency and amount for callback and webhook; string metadata and duplicate/out-of-order delivery are first-class cases. Durable captured-but-not-posted state with idempotent repair, never a lost payment or a false paid flag.
5. Full refund for paid, uncollected orders under an authorised staff workflow; unpaid cancellation releases allocations. Ambiguous provider responses stay in review until verified. No automatic retry of a possibly completed refund/transfer. Returns after collection, exchanges, partial refunds and restocking policy need an explicit separate agreement.
6. A dedicated, isolated provider sandbox and approved test recipient/operator are prerequisites to provider acceptance. Unit/fake-gateway tests do not prove actual charge/refund/payout delivery. No production credential swap. No new live messages or payments from this plan.

**Owner decisions needed:** store fee/bearer/cap; initial settlement mode and accountable refund/payout/support operator; collection/cancellation/return terms; real pilot products/organisation; isolated sandbox scope. Prepare the detailed failing-test-first S1b plan after these are settled, as required by the binding design §5/§9.

## Systematic sequence after those decisions

- **S1b:** shared order, line snapshot, payment attempt, allocation, reconciliation and receipt/history foundations; purchase adapter only. Prove last-item contention, lost responses, wrong provider/account/amount/currency, expired quotes, late/extra captures, ledger failure and permissions. No legacy venue migration.
- **S1c:** preparation/ready/collected workflow with a separate handover proof and one-time audit; collection permission distinct from finance/refunds. Sandbox purchase → receipt → collection, plus cancellation/refund and identity revocation. Publish support rules before a separately approved live pilot.
- **S2:** incrementally adapt venue transactions to common commerce while retaining authoritative VenueBooking/calendar records and old IDs/URLs. Design concurrency across every calendar writer, approval, bank-transfer evidence/manual review, deposits/balances, expiry, refunds and payout reconciliation. Preserve private staff rooms and booking-to-event/operations links.
- **S3:** quantity/asset rental availability over all overlapping intervals, buffers, collection/return/condition; service scope/approval/resource availability and acceptance. Use the common order/payment contracts with distinct reservation/fulfilment adapters. No permanent sale-stock deduction for rentals.
- **S4:** authenticated cross-organisation operations messaging and private attachments, tasks/inspections/calendar, walk-in external booker intake and consented/idempotent booking-to-event handoff. Prove each tenant boundary and repeated handoff.
- **S5:** trust/discovery and growth from the original handoff after accurate merchant participation and transaction/support readiness. No promotion of unconfirmed booking capability.

Each slice receives its own scoped plan, reviewable PR, affected checks and one final CI gate. Preserve current venue features throughout; no mixed-seller/type basket, destructive legacy conversion, automatic gateway change or broad tenant activation.
