# S1b shared commerce and merchandise checkout implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use `superpowers:executing-plans` natively, task by task, failing tests first. Tick steps in the implementation commit. One fresh whole-branch review after the author's self-review; no per-task review agents.

**Status:** owner approved native implementation on 2026-10-22; source implementation and acceptance review in progress on `codex/commerce-s1b`. No merge/deploy or production checkout activation authorised. Prepared after PR #49's approved merge/deployment (`b358f658`, 2026-10-22). Review the decisions below before building.

**Goal:** allow a verified buyer to purchase one organisation's merchandise, with bounded stock holds, correct payment/refund accounting and recoverable receipts; work for communities with no dues.

**Architecture:** extend the existing Shop/StoreListing catalog with durable StoreOrder/line snapshots, stock allocations, payment evidence and receipt/refund records. Keep payment, accounting and purchase fulfilment states separate. A reservation contract has only the purchase adapter in S1b; venue/rental/service adapters follow separately, without redirecting existing booking or payment paths.

**Tech stack:** current Laravel 13/PHP, PostgreSQL, Inertia/React, native verified-email access, existing gateway/ledger/queue/permission services. No dependency changes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-unified-commerce-design.md` §§2/5/6/7/8, amended by the proposed decisions in this review plan. This plan is a review proposal, not acceptance of those amendments.

## Decisions and recommendations for approval

1. **Pilot:** physical merchandise, GHS, collection only, one seller and purchase type per order; at most 20 distinct variants and quantity 1–99 per line. Public purchases require verified email but no membership, dues or Community. Private products require the existing active self/email membership when quoting. Phone proof, following and representative access never grant purchase or order-history access.
2. **Price/stock window:** propose 10 minutes from the server quote, with a visible countdown. Changing the cart creates a new quote; accepting/retrying never extends the window. Honour original price/member eligibility only for a valid accepted quote and verified capture within that window, with its allocation still held. Expired/released allocations or unknown/late capture time enter visible payment review rather than overselling. A mobile-money provider may complete after the app's timer; expiry is not a claim the provider cancelled the charge. Validate this timing in sandbox before release.
3. **Commission:** recommend seller-borne commission first, with no unexpected checkout surcharge. The owner must approve the rate/cap/bearer and tax/receipt presentation; do not adopt the old marketplace 10%, waive fees implicitly, infer them from an Event or invent tax amounts. A superadmin-approved, versioned store agreement is required before checkout. Null agreement means checkout unavailable; an explicitly stored 0.00 is a waiver. Tenants cannot edit percentage/cap; a bearer change is allowed only within the approved agreement.
4. **Settlement:** propose a platform-default Paystack sandbox pilot first only if the owner accepts responsibility for seller balances, refunds, support and payouts. Never change an organisation's settlement mode or credentials to fit this proposal. Own-gateway or other-provider tenants remain unavailable for this first slice until their separate account/split/commission-receivable adapter is approved; the shared attempt contract keeps original-account identity for that extension. If own-gateway-first is preferred, revise Tasks 2/4/6 before implementation.
5. **Financial isolation:** recommend a new `LedgerCategoryEnum::Commerce` with **event forbidden**, for new shared-commerce money. Leave legacy `Marketplace` balances untouched until S2 reconciliation. Otherwise old/unreconciled booking credits could become withdrawable merchandise proceeds. Reuse LedgerService and its chart/idempotency; no parallel ledger or fake Event. A later venue order may reference an event operationally, while the seller's commerce ledger remains independent of the booker's event.
6. **Cancellation/refunds:** unpaid cancellation releases a hold. Recommend staff-controlled full refund of paid, uncollected orders, with one audited restock only after the refund is confirmed and goods remain uncollected/resellable. Additional captures that never consumed stock must not restock. Refunds after collection, partial refunds, exchanges and damaged goods require an approved support policy before automation. Reserve refund liabilities before payouts; do not automatically transfer all seller funds immediately after payment.
7. **Release:** name the real pilot operator/products, collection/support contact and terms version. Agree isolated sandbox account/credentials and permitted recipients/actions separately. No production credential swap, live charge/refund/payout, public transaction promotion or rollout to other organisations as a side effect of accepting the plan.

## Proposed delivery increments

Keep the implementation native, but review it in three bounded increments: **A: Tasks 1–3**, durable orders/terms/stock holds with checkout off; **B: Tasks 4–6**, original-account payments, ledger/refund/payout integrity with activation off; **C: Tasks 7–8**, buyer/staff UX, receipts and sandbox acceptance. Create each model/migration in its owning task rather than shipping unused refund/receipt scaffolding in Task 1. Each increment must pass its affected checks; later increments depend on the earlier tested tree. Production trading waits for all increments and S1c collection acceptance. Do not treat an intermediate merge as launch approval.

## Global constraints

- Money is integer minor units. Validate checked sums/products against PHP integer and JavaScript-safe bounds; no client total, tenant ID, fee, account or provider reference controls a charge or fulfilment.
- Preserve event→tenant→package→config fee resolution and all existing finance invariants. Shared arithmetic belongs in FeeCalculator, posting in LedgerService. Keep the global superadmin gate; never authorize commission by an email address.
- Use `commerce_catalog` plus a separate exact `commerce_checkout` switch; disabled means no new quote/checkout. Recovery of money already received, refunds, receipts and reconciliation must still run when a switch is turned off.
- Preserve venue IDs/URLs/calendar, rooms, RFQs, quotes, operations, existing payment continuations, Community, dues, Giving and ticket verification. No mixed basket, shipping, discounts, pre-orders or new rental/service engine.
- Quotes, orders and receipts use native email proof and explicit DTOs/no-store; exclude them from ticket-only offline caches. No phone fallback, identifier-only access or raw model/credential serialization.
- No HTTP/provider calls while database inventory/accounting locks are held. Persist operation intent first, call provider outside locks, then reconcile. Ambiguous outcomes are not failures safe to repeat.
- Rebuilt `public/build` accompanies JS/CSS changes; permissions and role defaults together; `pint --dirty`; affected checks per task and one final CI gate on final source.

## Review Focus

1. A changed/reassigned phone, changed member email, expired proof or another tenant guessing an order ID must not reveal orders/receipts. Task 1/7 tests cover original verified-buyer ownership and private history restoration.
2. The last unit contested by two orders, a manual stock reduction, expiry and capture must never oversell or lose audit history. Task 3/5 uses two real connections and one lock order.
3. Provider init times out, callback and webhook race, account changes, fees are missing or a second capture arrives: money must remain recorded/reconcilable without duplicate orders, stock or ledger postings. Task 4/5.
4. A refund races collection/payout, or a transfer/refund succeeds but its response is lost: no double send, restock, collection or payment. Task 6; S1c must use the same locks for collection.
5. Existing legacy marketplace balances, a free item, seller-borne private rates, oversized arithmetic or disabled checkout must not accidentally produce a gateway charge, disclosure or payout. Task 2/4/6/8.

## File and data map

**New landlord models/factories/migrations:** `StoreCommercialAgreement`, `StoreOrder`, `StoreOrderLine`, `StoreStockAllocation`, `StorePaymentAttempt`, `StorePaymentObservation`, `StoreRefund`, `StoreReceipt`, `StoreOutboxMessage`. Use UUIDs and tenant/shop ownership constraints; restrictive historical references. Keep quoted totals/options/title/SKU/collection/terms immutable; no hard-deleting commerce/audit history.

- StoreOrder is also the immutable server quote, avoiding duplicate quote/line tables. `quoted` → `awaiting_payment` → `confirmed`; cancellation/review recorded explicitly. `payment_state` distinguishes not_required/awaiting/captured_review/paid/refund_pending/refunded; `ledger_state` distinguishes not_applicable/pending/posted/review. Purchase fulfilment is awaiting_preparation/ready/collected/cancelled, never inferred from paid. S1b does not expose ready/collected mutations; those belong to S1c.
- Lines snapshot listing/variant IDs, offering type, labels/options, quantity and integer unit/line amounts. The header freezes buyer email, seller, currency, totals, agreement/version/hash, collection/support terms, original gateway identity/mode and 10-minute expiry. A body-supplied email cannot replace native proof. No secret is stored in terms JSON.
- Allocation: variant/order/line, quantity, expires_at, active/released/consumed; unique line. Attempt: unique local reference, original account fingerprint/provider, init/verification/review fields and encrypted/hidden checkout URL. Unique provider/account/reference evidence; numeric Paystack ID is never the idempotency key.
- Observation: append-only verified evidence versions per original attempt/reference, with unique attempt/payload hash and a supersedes link, durably recorded before final ledger/stock transaction. Corrected missing evidence can be recorded without erasing history; a changed financial observation after posting is quarantined rather than rewriting the ledger. Refund/receipt/outbox records have unique order/capture/operation keys and retain retry outcomes. Never retain card, authorization or raw gateway secrets in diagnostics.

**Services:** existing `app/Services/Marketplace/` gains `StoreCommercialTerms`, `StoreOrderAccess`, `StoreQuotes`, `PurchaseReservations`, `StorePayments`, `StorePaymentEvidence`, `StorePaymentConfirmer`, `StoreRefunds`, `StoreReceipts`, `StoreBalance`, `StorePayouts`; contract `app/Contracts/StoreReservationAdapter.php`. Extend existing `MerchandiseInventory`, `FeeCalculator`, `LedgerService`, permissions/roles/routes and native personal workspace; do not repurpose event registration or contribution models.

## Provider/documentation checks

Checked 2026-10-22 against [Paystack transaction verification](https://paystack.com/docs/api/transaction/), [refund API](https://paystack.com/docs/api/refund/) and [official refund guidance](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127106). The refund API accepts a transaction reference/ID and merchant note; its refund-list filter is a transaction **ID**. That supports correlation/reconciliation, not an invented idempotency guarantee. Original processing fees are not returned according to the official guidance; cost/funding ownership therefore needs approval. Provider behavior still needs the separate sandbox acceptance. Laravel 13 Boost documentation checked transactional pessimistic locks/deadlock handling; use native connection transactions and after-commit outbox dispatch.
