> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans for the owner's established native approach. Implement task by task; write failing tests first and tick each completed step in the same commit as its work. **Goal:** Churches, volunteer groups and other organisations can collect voluntary contributions into funds and campaigns, online or as recorded cash, without dues, a membership type, a member register or a paid-ticket event.

**Architecture:** Giving has its own fund/gift records and rollout switch. Reuse CommercialTerms, gateway adapters, LedgerService, the existing Finance/payout infrastructure and the `/my` app. Keep EventContribution event-specific: a bounded association connects its existing records to funds; new event contributions switch accounting only through an explicit per-organisation cutover, frozen before checkout.

**Tech Stack:** PHP8.5, Laravel13, PostgreSQL/landlord connection, Pest5, Inertia/React, existing Tailwind4 tokens, existing Paystack and connected Stripe adapters. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-community-design.md` §§2,4,5.1–5.4,6.1–6.2,8,10,12–13.1,15; finance invariants in `AGENTS.md`. Read the roadmap and inbox design too; this plan does not change their identity rules.

## What the owner should review

The no-dues requirement is binding. The following details are **recommendations approved by the owner2026-10-17**:

- Use the familiar **Giving** tab in Community Money. Public copy can say **Contribute**, **Give an offering**, or **Support this project**, chosen by the organisation; never force church terminology onto a volunteer group. With no dues configuration/history, Giving is the default and zero arrears cards do not dominate Home/Overview. Dues remain optional and discoverable.
- Campaign progress uses contributed principal, including cash, less confirmed refunds/corrections. Gateway and platform fees are separately reported, not subtracted from the campaign bar. Default public progress is hidden until the organisation chooses amounts or percentage. No public donor-name wall in this milestone.
- A target does not close a campaign unless the organisation explicitly selects auto-close. A deadline stops new checkouts at the end of that local calendar day; already-started valid payments can still confirm. A callback is never rejected merely because a campaign has since closed.
- Recurring giving means an optional **email reminder** with a give link: weekly or monthly, default monthly after explicit consent. No auto-debit, promise to pay, unpaid balance, arrears or automatic member/follower creation. SMS giving reminders are deferred; existing announcements/SMS and dues reminders remain available under their existing rules.
- Provide core giving totals, receipts, date/fund/currency CSV and personal statements on every plan for this milestone. The spec's “basic/full” report distinction is not concrete enough to invent a new paid wall; richer analytics can be scoped separately. Keep existing negotiated terms and future pricing options intact.
- Refund recommendation: reverse platform commission proportionally from the recorded original commission, with cumulative rounding capped at that original amount; book only gateway fees/credits actually confirmed by the provider. Show the computed breakdown for approval before submitting a refund. No assumed refund of processing fees. This new-stream policy needs approval before implementation; historical event refund policy is preserved.
- Keep new online giving off until the operator confirms processor classification/readiness for the intended collection use. Do not infer that the user has approved real charges from approval to build. Existing event checkout providers remain available throughout cutover; no Stripe regression is acceptable.

## Global Constraints

- “Money is integer minor units (pesewas).” Only rates use decimal percentages. Convert validated decimal input once using exact decimal-string parsing, not floating-point multiplication. No mixed-currency totals or implicit FX.
- “LedgerService is the only writer of both ledgers.” `giving` is a separate ledger category; all references are idempotent per tenant/reference/type. Domain records report collections; ledgers report held money and payouts.
- Use `CommercialTerms::for(Tenant, CommercialStreamEnum::Giving)` and `commission()`: paid1%, Free/no-package2%, uncapped by default, organisation bears commission. An explicit0 override is a waiver. Fix Giving's existing no-package fallback, which currently falls through the contributions1% configuration. Do not change the tickets/event fee cascade.
- Only `access-superadmin-dashboard` may change commercial rate/cap, with existing audit history. Org admins cannot acquire this power by changing an email or role name. Never expose organisation-borne commission rates in public props.
- Freeze source/fund, amount, currency, provider/account/settlement mode and commercial terms before gateway initiation. Attempts store identifiers and frozen amounts, never copies of gateway secrets. Confirmation uses these snapshots and verified provider data; never today's rate or a browser-supplied success flag. Paystack identity is its reference; Stripe identity is the PaymentIntent on the original connected account.
- Recorded cash and bulk cash produce **no** ledger posting, commission, platform balance or payout. Corrections are appended and audited, not edits/deletions of posted gifts. Never record an off-platform cash amount as platform-held money.
- Existing event contributions keep records, references, receipt/tribute URLs, observer/webhook contracts and historic payouts/category. Never make `event_id` nullable on EventContribution. Never move historical event balances into Giving.
- Email remains primary. No global person table, phone-match discovery, inferred contact verification, membership or follow created by a gift. Member phone/representative access still requires the existing live member-specific grants.
- Public anonymity is not missing staff audit data: omit anonymous payer identity from payloads/CSV/PDF for anyone without `read finance`. Public pages have aggregate progress only, no contact details. The giver retains authorised access to their own record.
- Rollout, plan entitlements and staff permissions are distinct. New activity requires Community plus `community_giving`, never `member_dues`, member-cap space or a membership type. Disabling rollout or downgrading never removes existing receipts, reports, liabilities, refunds or payout operations.
- Permissions must be added to PermissionsSeeder and RolePermissions together; seed idempotently without restoring permissions an admin removed. No blanket permission grants to existing custom roles. Validate both route access and all serialised/CSV/PDF fields; permission to edit a public fund is not permission to read a private giver.
- Only ticket passes may be cached offline. Financial views/downloads are private/no-store and re-authorise every request. Use existing session blocking, fixed proof lifetimes, cross-tab clearing and current owner-generation protections.
- Preserve platform vs own-gateway settlement, commission credit/split accounting, payout locking, transfer fee pass-through and `awaiting_otp`. Use PayoutSender/PayoutRelease; never resend a parked transfer.
- All public request validation goes in FormRequest classes. Reuse existing console primitives and their actual props. No new framework, dependency or broad finance rewrite.
- Read version-specific docs through Boost before implementation; discover Artisan command signatures before use. Rebuild and commit `public/build` with every JS/CSS commit; `pint --dirty`. Focused tests per task, then one integration gate/CI run after implementation and review fixes.
- Implementation, merge/deploy and production cutover are separate approvals. Native work happens in the owned isolated worktree; original and Claude checkouts stay untouched. First approved rollout is Purpledot only; standing rollout approval does not authorise moving money, seeding live gifts, provider charges or historical cutover.

## Review Focus

1. **A church/volunteer group with zero members, types, events and dues** can publish a fund, receive a guest gift, record an offering batch and use Finance without creating any of those records (Tasks1,2,5,7).
2. **Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks, account changes and closing campaigns mid-payment** never duplicate gifts/ledgers, lose paid money or recalculate terms. A paid own-account gift never becomes platform-payable (Task3).
3. **A historical mixed ticket/contribution payout, including a pending payment at cutover**, remains byte-for-byte financially unchanged; the same contribution appears once in reports and `/my`, even across retries/rollback (Task6).
4. **Anonymous, cash-only and shared-phone givers** are not publicly identified, guessed into memberships or overcounted through transaction counts; recipient proofs and representative grants do not expand by association (Tasks2,5,7,8).
5. **Refunds after a payout, tiny balances, concurrent refunds/transfers and plan/flag changes** leave truthful per-currency collected/held/in-flight figures and operable recovery paths (Tasks3,4,7,9).

## Data and service contracts

These are proposed new names, not claims about existing code. All rows have UUIDs, tenant scope and timestamps; relevant money columns are bigint with non-negative/check constraints. Use foreign keys plus application validation to prevent cross-tenant associations. Add factories for models used in tests; no automatic production demo seeding.

- `giving_funds` / `GivingFund`: tenant, stable unique tenant/slug, name/description/button label, currency, kind `open|campaign`, target nullable, optional local `ends_on`, `close_on_target=false`, state `draft|open|closed|archived`, visibility `public|unlisted|private`, progress `hidden|percentage|amounts`, minimum amount, optional event_id, created_by. Default minimum is100 minor units for GHS, matching existing contribution minimum; validate other currencies against the existing connected provider configuration and do not assume FX or currency support. Reject precision beyond the currency's minor units; do not silently round a gift. Campaign requires positive target; an open fund has none. A fund's currency and event binding become immutable once attempts/collections exist; archive rather than delete history. Event-linked defaults are private; association never publishes a private event or its totals.
- `giving_gifts` / `GivingGift`: **standalone** fund gifts only; optional explicitly authorised member attribution, payer name/email/phone snapshots (restricted encrypted casts), normalised scoped payer_email_hash for exact-email history, anonymity, optional opaque giver key, original amount/currency, source `online|recorded_cash|bulk_cash`, status `pending|completed|failed|refunded`, recorded_by, paid_at/collected_at, original fee/commission/net, submission UUID. Bulk cash has no payer/member/giver key. No membership foreign key is required to give.
- `giving_contribution_links` / `GivingContributionLink`: unique event_contribution_id, tenant/fund, origin `legacy_association|checkout`, frozen accounting category `event|giving`, associated_at. EventContribution remains canonical for event-origin gifts; **do not create a duplicate GivingGift**. Legacy association does not update the source contribution, ledger or payout. Standalone visibility applies to the fund page/listing; an authorised existing event contribution uses the event's own public/private access and allowsContributions() rules plus an open fund, so its privately associated fund need not be published. Every reader deduplicates with a typed key `gift:{id}` or `event_contribution:{id}`.
- `giving_payment_attempts` / `GivingPaymentAttempt`: exactly one of gift_id/event_contribution_id; tenant/fund, submission UUID, expected principal/currency, rate/cap/bearer/source/`commission_amount`, provider, immutable gateway/account identity and settlement mode, commission split/credit snapshot, local reference, provider session/payment reference, category snapshot, state (`created|initialising|open|initialisation_failed|needs_review|confirmed`) and confirmed_at. A local cancel/timeout does not negate a later verified paid result. Unique source submission and unique provider/account/reference where set. Provider initialisation failures/unknown outcomes remain auditable; a retry reuses the idempotency identity rather than starting another charge blindly.
- `giving_adjustments` / `GivingAdjustment`: typed source, tenant/fund, reason, principal reduction, original-commission reversal/actual gateway credit, kind `refund|reversal|cash_correction`, status `requested|processing|confirmed|failed|unknown`, actor/time, unique operation UUID and provider refund reference. Sum of confirmed reductions cannot exceed original principal; lock source and the applicable payable account. A cash correction never invokes a gateway or ledger.
- `giving_receipt_notices` / `GivingReceiptNotice`: typed source/payment key, immutable receipt number/issued snapshot, delivery destination, queue state; unique receipt/delivery key. Queue after the successful transaction; no duplicate receipts on callback replay. Original receipt is preserved and adjustments have their own notes. Restrict this table's destination/receipt snapshot fields like the gift itself; queue delivery only to a real recorded address. Use ordinary “Contribution receipt” wording, matching existing donation receipt tests.
- `giving_reminders` / `GivingReminder`: tenant/fund, independently verified email encrypted plus scoped hash, explicit purpose consent timestamp, weekly/monthly cadence, anchor day (ISO weekday or day-of-month), local time (default09:00), timezone/next_due_at, disabled_at, hashed unsubscribe token. Delivery dedupe key is reminder+consent epoch+due period. Default monthly anchor is day1; weekly defaults to the organisation's week-start day. Clamp a requested monthly day31 to February's last day without changing the stored31 anchor for March. No debtor, charge, subscription mandate or member is created.
- Add nullable `giving_fund_id` attribution to ledger transactions through LedgerService, validated against tenant/category. Giving accounts/payouts are **event-less**, pooled by tenant/category/currency, as dues already are. Event/fund context stays on source/link records. Never present a fund's raised total as its own withdrawable balance. An event's new giving is labelled “Paid out through Finance > Giving”, not added to its historical event-payable pool.
- Extend CommunitySettings with nullable per-tenant `giving_event_cutover_at` (UTC), set only by the explicit cutover operation. New payments freeze their category at initiation; pre-cutover pending rows stay legacy even if confirmation is later.

**Service interfaces used between tasks:**
- `GivingRollout::enabled(Tenant): bool`; `onlineEnabled(Tenant): bool` additionally requires explicit online readiness metadata; `Tenant::hasGivingMoney(): bool` includes source history, adjustments, giving accounts and payouts; finance/history gates use that even with rollout off.
- `GivingFunds::save(Tenant, User, array $validated, ?GivingFund): GivingFund`; `GivingFunds::close(GivingFund, User, string $reason): void` (reopen/archive are similarly audited actions, never erase money).
- `GivingSources::find(Tenant, string $type, string $id): GivingSource` yields a readonly value with typed source identity, fund, principal, currency and source model; `GivingSources::lock(Tenant,string $type,string $id): GivingSource` re-reads under transaction lock. Only the two named types are allowed; never accept an arbitrary PHP class from metadata.
- `GivingCash::record(GivingFund, User, array $validated, string $submissionKey): GivingGift`; `correct(GivingGift, User, int $reduction, string $reason, string $operationKey): GivingAdjustment`.
- `GivingProgress::for(GivingFund): array{raised:int,left:int|null,percent:float|null,identified_givers:int,target_reached:bool}`; a campaign may exceed100%, left is floored0 and visual bar capped100%; copy shows the overage. Open funds return left=null, percent=null and target_reached=false: no target/bar or division by zero. No rates or contacts in public output.
- `GivingCheckout::start(GivingFund, array $validated, Session $session, string $submissionKey, ?EventContribution $eventSource=null): string` returns hosted URL; `GivingPaymentConfirmer::confirm(string $reference, array $verifiedData): ?GivingPaymentAttempt` returns confirmed attempt or null for quarantined/unmatched data; a duplicate returns the existing confirmed attempt without re-posting.
- `LedgerService::recordGivingPayment(Tenant, GivingSource, GivingPaymentAttempt, array $verifiedAmounts): LedgerTransaction`, `recordGivingRefund(Tenant, GivingSource, GivingAdjustment): LedgerTransaction`, `recordGivingPayout(Tenant, EventPayout, string $reference, int $transferFee): LedgerTransaction`. Cash never calls these. Own-account posting uses actual split/commission credit through the existing settlement helpers, not fabricated gross cash on the platform.
- `GivingBalance::{payable,inFlight,available}(Tenant,string $currency): int`, `lockPayableAccount(Tenant,string): LedgerAccount`; available = max(0,payable−inFlight−pendingRefundReservations); take the same account lock for all reservations and read commitments before payable for conservative unlocked summaries. `inFlight` remains payout-only; expose pending refunds separately so Finance does not silently rename a payout figure. Expose a negative payable as a separately labelled amount owed, never delete/clamp the ledger itself.
- `GivingRefunds::request(GivingSource,User,int $principal,string $reason,string $operationKey): GivingAdjustment`; `confirm(GivingAdjustment,array $verifiedOutcome): void` owns final reduction/ledger posting. Provider failure/unknown leaves a visible pending review, not a claimed successful refund.
- `GivingReport::summary(Tenant,CarbonImmutable $from,CarbonImmutable $to,array $filters=[]): array` returns groups by currency, separate online/cash/adjustment lines and independent giving balance; `export(Tenant,CarbonImmutable $from,CarbonImmutable $to,array $filters=[]): StreamedResponse` streams CSV with formula protection.
- `GivingHistory::for(Session,array $filters): LengthAwarePaginator`; `receipt(Session,string $type,string $id): array`; `statement(Session,Tenant,array $filters): array`. Current proofs and explicit grants are checked on each call, including PDF download. Giver-facing receipts never expose another payer's contacts merely because a gift was attributed to the represented member.
- `GivingContributionBridge::associate(Tenant,bool $apply=false): array`; `reconcile(Tenant): array{mismatches:list<string>,financial_snapshot:array<string,mixed>,source_keys:list<string>}`; `cutover(Tenant,CarbonImmutable $at,bool $apply=false): array`; source selection never uses confirmation time to reclassify an old payment.
- `GivingReceipts::issue(GivingSource,string $paymentKey): GivingReceiptNotice` persists/queues the immutable deduped receipt only after its source transaction commits.
- `GivingReminders::subscribe(Tenant,GivingFund,Session,array $input): GivingReminder`; `dispatchDue(CarbonImmutable): void`; `unsubscribe(string $token): void`.
