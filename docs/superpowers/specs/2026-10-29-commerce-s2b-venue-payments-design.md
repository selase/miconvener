# S2b Venue Payments and Reconciliation Design

**Date:** 29 October 2026. **Status:** proposed for owner review; no implementation, merge, deployment, activation or money movement authorised.

**Read with:** the marketplace roadmap, unified-commerce design, S2a plan and current AGENTS.md finance/calendar invariants. S2a is merged as PR #58 (`a738dba9`) and its automatic Cloud deployment succeeded. Approval and venue-payment rollout remain separate; Purpledot merchandise is already live and must remain unaffected.

## 1. Outcome and scope

After the host approves dates, the host issues a final financial quote. The verified booker accepts its exact rental, extras, payment schedule, refundable security amount and cancellation terms. Successful, verified money confirms the booking only if the applicable calendar conditions still hold. Buyer and host see deposit/balance/security separately, with a receipt for each capture and an aggregate statement that does not count refunded money twice.

S2b includes hosted Paystack GHS payments through the supported platform account, bounded full-capture refunds, protected security money, manual seller payouts and explicit reconciliation of legacy payment/calendar exceptions. S2c adds bank-transfer instructions, pro-forma/evidence and manual payment verification; S3/S4/S5 remain later work. No own-gateway migration, direct gateway split, subscriptions, arbitrary instalment plans, partial capture, discounts, damage deduction, card-data collection or automatic payout is included.

**Proposed execution checkpoints:** A — schema, adapter/locks and quote/charge lifecycle, still no activation; B — verification, reconciliation, security/refunds/payout controls; C — UI/acceptance/review. Do not ship a payments-on intermediate release before B/C pass. Reviewable smaller PRs may deploy with the venue-payment flag off.

## 2. Current code constraints that drive the design

- `StoreOrder` and line database checks currently allow purchase only; lines require a genuine merchandise variant. `StorePayments` requires stock or a production quote and a collection/delivery arrangement. `StorePaymentConfirmer`, `StorePaymentNotifications`, `StoreRefunds`, `StoreReceipts`, order screens and `StoreBalance` contain purchase-specific assumptions. A venue link alone will not make them generic.
- `CommerceInventoryLocks` locks tenant → order → variants. `VenueCalendarLocks` locks listing → booking. Combining them without a top-level contract can deadlock. Every new charge/evidence/refund/resolution writer must use the common lock ordering in §5.
- Common provider verification already handles string metadata, original account identity, amount/currency/reference, immutable observations, duplicate capture and review liabilities. Preserve it instead of cloning a second verifier.
- Legacy `VenueBookingService` still has its older fee/ledger/payment integration. S2a conflict actions store received reference/amount/currency evidence, not verified financial truth. Replayed conflicts intentionally stay unresolved. `MarketplaceLedger` uses the `marketplace` ledger category; current shared commerce uses `commerce`.
- Existing full-capture refunds and merchant balance protection can be reused after typed adaptation. Their current inventory/restock/cancellation effects must never run on a venue or on a security-only refund.

## 3. Commercial decisions proposed for review

1. **Final price and currency:** GHS integer pesewas; explicit itemised rental and non-refundable extras. An S2a indicative total is not a payable quote. Quote expiry is the earlier of the approved hold deadline and event start. Host cannot issue against a conflicted, closed, paid-under-another-contract or expired hold. Withdrawal/replacement is allowed only before acceptance and without an unresolved attempt; after any capture, financial amendments require a separately designed workflow, not an edit.
2. **Booking deposit versus security:** booking deposit is part-payment of the rental; security is a distinct refundable liability. Host explicitly chooses full rental payment or an integer deposit amount between 1 pesewa and the rental total, with one balance deadline before the event. Do not silently inherit legacy 25%/GH₵50 defaults. If the deposit equals the rental total, no zero balance checkout is created.
3. **Schedule:** the initial rental payment secures confirmation; the balance is payable later against the same accepted contract. A balance becoming overdue is a staff warning, not an automatic cancellation or extra charge. No recurring debit. Security, when required, is a separate payment due before access to the venue; its payment does not itself confirm the venue. A wholly zero-price contract follows explicit no-payment acceptance and calendar confirmation, with no fake gateway or cash entry.
4. **Commission:** recommend the owner's 3% sales policy, adjustable only through an approved tenant agreement; tenant bears gateway, refund-related processing, payout and transfer fees. Propose commission on rental plus non-refundable extras, excluding refundable security. Use a **reservation-scoped** approved commercial agreement. Add immutable `offering_scope` to StoreCommercialAgreement (existing rows default to purchase), keep global per-shop version numbering, and make resolve/approve explicitly scope-aware with purchase as the backward-compatible default. Venue support/cancellation/rate changes must not become the latest purchase agreement or alter Purpledot merchandise terms. No new buyer surcharge. This basis and its application to venues require owner review; never overwrite historical legacy or merchandise terms. Use FeeCalculator/FeeTerms, including explicit zero waivers and nullable caps.
5. **Rounding/caps:** calculate total rental commission once from the accepted contract. Allocate the initial instalment's fee using FeeCalculator (not more than the contract fee); allocate the exact remainder to the balance. A cap applies once per contract, not separately per charge. Receipt/refund reversals use those stored allocations. Security commission is zero by type, not a host-supplied fee override.
6. **Cancellation and refund:** quote must contain the host's actual cancellation/refund terms and support contact; no invented universal percentage/cutoff. Initial implementation supports full refunds of individual captured instalments and full security returns. Any partially retained rental/security, exchanges or damage claim needs an explicit later plan. Recommend reversing the stored commission on refunded rental money, with original non-returned gateway fees borne by the tenant. No automatic refund merely because a hold expired.
7. **Payout release:** recommend holding venue earnings until the booked period ends and an authorised completion decision is recorded, with no open reconciliation/refund/dispute. Payout is still manual and retains provider OTP protection. Earlier seller release requires an explicit approved policy change, not a host checkbox. Security never becomes available seller earnings, even after the event; it must be returned or remain held pending an agreed dispute-resolution feature.
8. **Scope/launch:** first support approved platform-default settlement only, no credential switch. Separate `marketplace_venue_payments` flag requires request rollout, actual merchant agreement and appropriate permissions, and defaults off. `commerce_checkout` continues to control purchases only. Do not enable venues or alter Purpledot merchandising as a side effect of schema deployment.

## 4. Native model: one contract, explicit charges, common payment records

Retain `VenueBooking` as the sole calendar/operational source. Add immutable `VenueFinancialQuote` versions owned by the same tenant/shop/booking, with exact dates, amounts, contract fee allocation, cancellation/support terms, currency/account identity, hold version/deadline, balance/security deadlines, issuer and request hash. Acceptance/withdrawal/completion are separate append-only decisions, not snapshot edits. At most one accepted contract per booking; paid amendments are deferred.

`VenueBookingCharge` links the accepted contract to one common `StoreOrder` for each purpose: `rental_initial`, optional `rental_balance`, optional `security`, or exceptional `legacy_review`. Each charge is one full amount; buyer cannot choose a smaller value. Uniqueness `(quote_id, purpose)` plus order/tenant/shop composite constraints prevents duplicate instalments or foreign links. Initial/balance totals equal the contract rental, and security is excluded from that sum. An accepted contract can create later-due orders without permitting checkout before their eligibility; issuance and acceptance must not manufacture a receipt.

Extend `offering_type` to `reservation`. Venue order lines reference the real venue listing and have no merchandise variant; nullable variant is legal only for a constrained reservation line with a valid charge/booking source. No fake SKU, stock movement, collection arrangement or Event is created. Use explicit nullable/composite/type constraints and immutability triggers, not a global removal of purchase checks. Existing purchase, made-to-order and historical order shapes stay unchanged.

Each charge uses existing `StorePaymentAttempt`, verified `StorePaymentObservation`, `StoreReceipt` and provider refund references. Add typed adapter dispatch rather than `if venue then skip validation`. Register purchase and venue implementations explicitly; unknown or inconsistent types fail closed. Shared order pages/receipts identify the booking and instalment, while the existing private booking dossier is the aggregate entry point. No merchandise collection pass is issued for a venue payment.

## 5. Locking, network calls and confirmation

New money operations lock **tenant → listing → booking → financial quote/charge → StoreOrder → attempt/observation/refund**, and lock additional rows in stable UUID order. Purchase operations keep tenant → order → variants. S2a calendar-only writers remain listing → booking and must not call an operation that then acquires a tenant lock. Introduce a typed `CommerceOrderLocks` entry point; update all shared money paths, not just checkout, so callbacks/refunds cannot invert the venue ordering. Claim provider operations under locks; release locks before network calls; reacquire and verify the original claim afterwards.

A buyer's exact accepted contract hash, verified email and charge identity are checked before an attempt. Public/MCP/host legacy entry points cannot create an alternative payable reservation for a new approval-flow booking. Unresolved provider initialisation prohibits another attempt; verification/continuation reuses the original reference rather than issuing another charge.

A successful initial rental capture confirms only when account/reference/metadata/amount/currency/timestamp match; its provider `paid_at` is before quote/hold expiry and event start; the contract was accepted; the booking is still compatible and the slot remains free under the shared calendar lock. Delayed delivery of timely payment can confirm if those checks pass; a newly occupied slot can never be displaced. Ledger post and reservation confirmation commit together. Posting failure rolls back paid/confirmed state while retaining durable captured evidence for retry/review. Later rental balance/security captures validate the accepted confirmed booking, their own due/expiry rules and exact charge, not an already-expired initial hold.

Expired initial checkout never silently renews a hold. Late, extra, wrong-amount/account/currency, closed-booking or conflicting captures enter non-payable review with reserved funds. A paid booking cannot be cancelled through the S2a unpaid-release route. Readiness/fulfilment, financial payment and security-return states remain distinct.

## 6. Legacy conflict evidence and ledger reconciliation

Expose a finance-only work queue for `payment_calendar_conflict` and other captured-review cases, scoped to owning tenant and the approved original provider account. Re-verify from the server. Stored metadata, an uploaded screenshot or a matching email is not proof of money.

Before posting, resolve the global ownership of `(provider account, reference)` across legacy bookings, existing ledger entries and shared attempts/observations. Existing `MKT-{booking reference}-{provider reference}` postings must be linked, not copied into a second ledger stream. Preserve their original category/rate/amount and statement history. Never finance a commerce payout from a legacy marketplace credit merely by relabelling it. Legacy posted-money payout/refund needs a proven category-aware adapter or remains explicitly blocked for staff reconciliation; it is not closed by recording an audit comment.

For verified money never posted, add an authorised, idempotent import path with origin `legacy_reconciliation`. It records the actual server-verified reference/capture and original evidence link without backdating a new attempt, forging acceptance or treating the verified amount as an agreed sale price. Extend evidence timestamp rules for that explicit source using the original booking chronology, not a global relaxation. Unknown original commercial terms or expected amount force review/refund-only until resolved, never automatic sale confirmation at the new 3% rate.

Resolution is an explicit append-only staff command: (a) retain for investigation; (b) refund verified captured money with funding checks; or (c) confirm only after original/renewed buyer consent, provable financial terms, policy eligibility and a fresh locked availability check. The simplest launch recommendation is **refund-only for ambiguous historical captures**. Confirmation must not be inferred from a replay or a cancelled competitor. Existing evidence is never edited or deleted. No “mark paid”, paid-flag override, arbitrary-amount credit, automatic charge or automatic provider retry is offered.

## 7. Ledger, refunds and payout reserves

Agreement resolution and current holdback policy reads must filter the correct offering scope; payout protection still includes every applicable snapshotted order reserve without double counting. All entries go through `LedgerService`, reference-idempotent and tenant/currency/category scoped. Rental captures post net clearing, the stored allocated platform commission and tenant payable. Security captures credit a dedicated refundable-security liability for the full refundable principal; actual gateway fees are charged to the tenant's position so buyer security is not silently reduced. A tenant funding deficit is visible and blocks payout/security return submission until covered; no negative transfer or cross-tenant cash borrowing.

Security liability must be deducted from available **cash** as well as excluded from seller payable. Otherwise it could incorrectly fund a different merchandise payout within the same tenant/category. `StoreBalance::available`, `cashBuffer`, refund funding, in-flight payout reservations and payout release/reconciliation must all account for it. Unreleased venue earnings likewise reserve payoutable funds, without unnecessarily blocking backed refunds. Reserves are not expenses, revenue or a second liability posted on top of existing payable; choose one auditable representation and test it end-to-end.

Refund state is provider-confirmed, with native unknown-outcome protection and the original capture/amount/reference. Tenant funds actual fees. Security return does not cancel a paid rental or restock anything; rental cancellation releases calendar occupancy according to policy and its own recorded decision, not a purchase restock flag. Rental, balance and security receipts/statements show amounts paid, outstanding, refunded and held accurately. Never claim a refund complete when merely queued. Payout fees are recognised only when actually released; OTP-parked payouts reuse their original reference.

## 8. Permissions, UI and acceptance

Keep host quote creation under existing `manage venue` pricing authority; commission/cap/bearer remain application-superadmin-owned. Add distinct `reconcile venue payments`, `manage venue refunds`, `manage venue payouts` permissions in PermissionsSeeder and RolePermissions together, initially only global Superadmin/Org Superadmin. No automatic extension of merchandise-finance permission to venue money. Custom roles must be explicit and tenant-bounded.

Buyer dossier shows final/indicative distinction, accepted contract version, initial payment/balance/security cards, deadlines, paid/overdue/review/refund labels, exact receipts and contact details. Verification return uses the strict shared helper. Amounts/status refresh from the server on return/focus; expiry never leaves a stale Pay button. Finance queue shows source reference, verified versus received evidence, ledger linkage, available funding and guarded resolution actions. No gateway codes/secrets or internal commission rate in seller-borne public payloads. Existing staff calendar and event collaboration remain authoritative and private.

Acceptance requires targeted PHP/JS tests, real-worker races, browser checks at desktop/390px/dark/keyboard/stale tab, one fresh whole-change review and one final CI gate. Provider sandbox acceptance is separately approved and never swaps production credentials. Prove deposits, balance, security return, duplicate/out-of-order delivery, held/late money, ledger failure/recovery, refund/payout funding and OTP, plus historical money not reposting and live merchandise isolation. Feature remains off until explicit merchant/financial/release approval; the earlier GH₵1 merchandise tests do not certify venue trading.

## Owner review decisions

Approve/amend: explicit host-entered deposit and balance due date; commission excludes refundable security and cap applies once; full-capture-only refunds/security returns with tenant fees; payout release after event completion; refund-only default for ambiguous legacy captures; platform collection first and separate venue activation. These are recommendations, not inferred commercial consent.
