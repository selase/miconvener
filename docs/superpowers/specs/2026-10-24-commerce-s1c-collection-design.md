# S1c — Collection, delivery and handover design

**Status:** revised 2026-10-24 following the owner’s collection/delivery clarification; planning only, no application implementation or activation. This revision supersedes the earlier collection-only scope. Prepared 2026-10-24 against `main` merge `7a90c66c` (PR #50). The owner has approved native implementation; this document incorporates the requested pre-implementation amendment.

## 1. Purpose and current state

Finish the merchandise journey: the buyer chooses collection or seller-arranged delivery from the seller’s offered options; the seller prepares the full order and authorised staff explicitly confirm the final handover. A ready order remains available until collected or delivered, unless explicitly cancelled/refunded through the existing safeguards. No event association is required. This works for public merchandise and church, volunteer-group or association stores without requiring dues or Community membership. Private-product eligibility stays in the existing quote flow; later membership lapse does not confiscate a paid order.

S1b is deployed: immutable quotes/lines/terms, original-account payments, stock consumption, receipts/history, full uncollected refunds and payouts. Its `fulfilment_state` already permits `awaiting_preparation`, `ready`, `collected`, `cancelled`, but it exposes no ready/collection mutation. Receipt delivery is already recoverable. Keep those contracts and all venue/event/Community flows.

## 2. Recommended scope and decisions for review

Owner instruction: ready orders remain available until collected or delivered, and buyers choose from the options offered by the seller. The operational defaults below are implementation recommendations; commercial/trading activation still requires separate approval:

- **Complete-order collection or seller-arranged delivery.** All purchased lines/quantities are handed over together using the buyer’s chosen offered method. Collection may be at an association office or another stated collection point. Delivery ends at the agreed buyer address. No partial fulfilment, unattended drop-off, return/exchange, offline completion, courier integration or staff bypass of recipient proof.
- **Pass possession authorises handover.** A pass is a bearer credential issued only to the original verified buyer. We cannot prove physical identity without adding another identity process. Copy: “Anyone you share this pass with can collect your entire order.” Do not collect ID documents/photos or treat matching a phone/email typed by staff as proof. This is an explicit purchaser-authorised bearer handover, not representative membership access.
- **Separate purpose, online only.** Collection pass is not an event ticket, admission QR, receipt number or public order number. Camera scanning and manual entry are alternative inputs to the same server verifier; neither hands goods over automatically.
- **15-minute verification, no order deadline.** Readiness starts no expiry clock: the order stays ready for days or longer until explicit completion/cancellation. Generate the pass only when the collector is present or delivery reaches the recipient; never generate it automatically on ready or dispatch. Expiry affects verification only, never the order, payment, stock allocation or receipt. One current pass per order; server expiry is authoritative. Original verified email can refresh it, revoking its predecessor. Email/proof expiry prevents new issue and clears the screen; already issued bearer proof remains valid only until its own 15-minute expiry unless revoked/consumed or order/financial eligibility changes. Tell the buyer this boundary; do not pretend an issued screenshot can be recalled from another device.
- **Staff permission separate from money.** New `manage store collection` authorises preparation and handover, not refunds, payout, prices or manual paid status. Recommend built-in Org Superadmin and Org Admin defaults, plus tenant-safe custom roles; ordinary event check-in staff do not inherit it.
- **Ready email is transactional.** One immutable readiness notice per accepted ready transition, with a native order link, accepted method-specific fulfilment/support terms and no pass secret or delivery address. Delivery failure never changes readiness or prevents a verified buyer reading their pass in My orders. No SMS or marketing messages in this slice.
- **Independent pause switch.** New exact `commerce_collection`, default off. When on, old paid/free orders can be prepared/collected even with catalog/checkout off. When off, no new ready/pass/validation/collection actions; history, receipts, financial recovery and support remain readable/operable. Re-enabling does not renew old pass expiry. Production activation needs a named operator and owner approval.

## 2a. Seller offers and buyer choice (owner clarification)

- Add a versioned `StoreFulfilmentPolicy` per shop, managed by `manage store catalog`: enabled methods `collection` and/or `delivery`, collection location/hours/instructions, delivery coverage/instructions and expected timing. Require at least one method; do not silently enable delivery. Publication/quote checks intersect shop methods across every merchandise line. Preserve existing collection instructions/terms; a delivery-only listing does not require a fictitious collection location.
- Buyer chooses a single common method for the whole order before accepting the quote. If only one is offered, show it explicitly and require acceptance. No common method means separate orders, not a hidden split. Seller withdrawal/changes affect new quotes only; accepted arrangements retain their versioned terms. The order remains independent of event, membership and dues.
- Record buyer choice and accepted policy snapshot in a one-to-one `StoreFulfilmentArrangement` under tenant/order locks, without rewriting the S1b order’s immutable quote/financial fields. A choice fingerprint participates in quote reuse/idempotency: same cart with a changed method/address/terms cannot replay stale delivery consent. Payment initialisation requires an accepted arrangement; already-issued historical quotes/orders stay readable and must use an explicit buyer selection from a versioned seller offer before readiness. Never mutate a pending provider attempt to change its arrangement.
- Delivery requires recipient name and a structured destination (address lines, locality, country, optional postal code/landmark, instructions); phone is optional logistical contact, never identity proof. Enforce bounded lengths and supported coverage before acceptance. Encrypt destination/contact, exclude from logs, telemetry, URLs, ordinary collection lists and email; show only to the verified buyer and collection-authorised staff opening that order’s delivery detail. Revalidate access each time, private/no-store responses. No address in immutable public audit; preserve restricted encrypted accepted address separately.
- **Bounded first release recommendation:** seller-arranged delivery is included in the displayed merchandise price, with no separate delivery charge, cash-on-delivery or courier collection of payment. Show this before acceptance. Sellers needing a separate fee cannot offer delivery until a quoted fee/accounting extension is approved; do not improvise an off-platform surcharge. Provider/courier integrations, distance pricing and tracking remain later work.
- Buyer can choose/change method and destination while quoted (replace the immutable quote/arrangement, explicitly accept, transfer the existing reservation atomically and preserve the original hold expiry). A pending provider attempt prevents replacement until safely resolved; never create parallel stock holds or renew the deadline. After acceptance, the arrangement is frozen. Any necessary paid-order change is a support case; no silent seller switch, price change or unverified address edit. Historical orders may establish an initial arrangement once with verified buyer consent; support cannot force it.
- Ready has **no automatic expiry, disposal, cancellation or refund**. Explicit customer-support and existing cancellation/refund actions remain possible before dispatch/handover. No new storage deadline is inferred. Collection credentials may be renewed at arrival; delivery credentials are issued by the original verified buyer and shared with their authorised recipient at arrival. Do not send a pass to the courier or treat courier pickup as buyer receipt. Warn that anyone given a code can authorise receipt of the entire order.
- Reuse the 15-minute proof mechanism with distinct payload purpose `miconvener.store.delivery.v1` for delivery and the existing collection purpose for pickup. Bind proof to the accepted method and current readiness/dispatch generation; wrong-mode proof is rejected. One completion record per order across both methods. The `commerce_collection` feature and `manage store collection` permission remain internal compatibility identifiers covering physical fulfilment; UI labels say **Store fulfilment**. Neither grants finance access.

## 3. Financial and fulfilment state contract

Eligible means `state=confirmed`, `payment_state=paid` with `ledger_state=posted`, or `payment_state=not_required` with zero charged total and `ledger_state=not_applicable`; no `review_reason`, no unresolved/captured-review attempt, pending observation or non-confirmed primary-order refund, and original line allocations all consumed. A confirmed extra-capture refund does not alone block collection once review has legitimately cleared; S1c must not clear financial review itself. No financial-review override is added; existing additional-capture review can remain blocked for finance/support even after its refund completes. Do not promise automatic resumption or silently clear changed-verification evidence.

- `awaiting_preparation → ready`: collection permission, enabled collection switch and eligibility. Preserve original collection/support terms; no price, stock or ledger write. Record actor/time and immutable line/terms identity. Require a buyer-selected fulfilment arrangement; do not infer consent to collection for historical orders.
- `ready → awaiting_preparation`: “Return to preparation,” required reason (1–500 characters), same ownership/permission and lock; revoke active passes and record an immutable event. No email is promised for this reversal. A subsequent ready transition has a new event/notice; browser retries do not make another transition/email.
- `ready → collected`: valid unexpired current pass, enabled collection switch, collection permission and current eligibility, explicit staff confirmation that all goods are being handed over. Atomically consume the pass, record one collection and move fulfilment to collected. **No second stock deduction or financial posting**: S1b already consumed stock at confirmed purchase.
- Delivery branch: `ready → dispatched → delivered`; dispatch is not receipt and uses no recipient code. Dispatch requires delivery mode, current eligibility and an immutable destination/instructions snapshot. Final delivered confirmation requires a current recipient pass and explicit whole-order handover at the destination, under the same locks and idempotency rules as collection. An unsuccessful attempt records a reason and remains dispatched; no automatic completion, refund or restock. A separately audited physical return to seller permits `dispatched → ready`, revokes passes and retains all attempt history; only then may pre-handover cancellation/refund resume. Return-to-preparation is unavailable while dispatched.
- `collected` and `delivered` are terminal for this slice. Preserve immutable actor/name/time/source/pass-ID and line snapshot evidence; no uncollect/delete. Collected-order refunds/returns stay an explicit support policy gap, not an unsafe button.
- Cancellation/refund keeps S1b's finance permission, funded-money checks and confirmed-restock rules. A primary refund/free cancellation is denied while dispatched or after collected/delivered; stock already outside seller custody must not be restocked automatically. Before dispatch/handover, starting a primary refund immediately blocks collection/delivery and invalidates the pass, including `needs_funding` or an unknown provider outcome. Free cancellation does likewise. An extra capture refund must not cancel/restock the valid primary order or revoke a valid pass solely because its separate refund exists; its unresolved financial review still blocks handover.

**One lock order:** Tenant row → Order row → sorted variant advisory/row locks only when the existing refund needs stock. All collection/pass/readiness/refund claim/reversal writers use `CommerceInventoryLocks`; network and mail calls remain outside locks. Dispatch/collection/delivery-first makes the primary refund request fail; refund-first makes dispatch or handover fail. Durable provider completion is still recorded even if an impossible historical/manual collected state is discovered; show support review, never hide money or restock handed-over goods.

## 4. Proof, idempotency and retention

`StoreCollectionPass` belongs to order/tenant/shop with composite ownership constraints. Store UUID, version, issue/expiry/revocation/consumption timestamps, issuing buyer-email snapshot, SHA-256 verifier hashes and encrypted hidden material sufficient to replay the same successful issue request after a lost response. Do not store a session ID or raw native verification code.

Generate independent 256-bit random QR secret and 12-character random uppercase Crockford Base32 manual code (60 bits; alphabet `0123456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ`); manual input strips spaces/hyphens, uppercases and maps O→0 and I/L→1, then accepts exactly 12 alphabet characters. QR is a purpose-tagged data payload `{purpose,pass_id,secret}` with `miconvener.store.collection.v1` for pickup or `miconvener.store.delivery.v1` for delivery; **not a navigable URL**. Manual submission includes the seller's order number/ID, so code lookup is scoped to the authorised seller/order. Reject other QR purposes, oversized input (>2 KiB), invalid UUID/version, unexpected properties and malformed code before lookup. Hash comparisons are constant-time. Secrets never appear in URLs, normal order DTOs, email, logs, Telescope, analytics, browser storage or ticket offline caches.

Issue is POST-only under live original-email proof. A UUID operation key replays the same still-active credential/expiry for the same request; after expiry/revocation, replay gives “pass expired/replaced,” not a new credential. A fresh operation key explicitly rotates; throttle issuance 5 per buyer/order per 10 minutes. At most one unconsumed/unrevoked pass per order; rotation and issue are under the same order lock.

Staff validation is a throttled POST: 5 failed credential attempts per staff+order per 10 minutes, plus 30/minute per staff+tenant and existing authentication/CSRF. Unknown and foreign IDs fail generically and do not disclose products/buyer. Use an HMAC of submitted selectors in limiter keys, not raw secrets. First validate actor/tenant before loading proof. Validation returns only matched seller order, masked buyer email, snapshotted lines, collection terms and a can-confirm result; it does not consume or create a reservation. Confirm resubmits proof + operation key and rechecks everything under locks; a successful scan does not guarantee later confirmation.

Collection or delivery completion shares one `StoreCollection` handover record with immutable method `collection|delivery` (the legacy class name does not imply an event or collection-only UI). Completion has unique order ID and unique order-scoped operation key. A retry by still-authorised staff using the original consumed proof/key returns the same immutable collection even after that proof expires. Reuse with altered proof/order is denied; another operator/request cannot create a second collection. A different request with the same valid consumed pass reports “Already collected” or “Already delivered” with permitted seller audit; an unrelated wrong credential remains generic. Completed evidence is retained and immutable, including cancelled/expired/revoked pass history; revoke/redact encrypted proof material after it is no longer needed while retaining hashes, IDs and audit. A scheduled purge (daily) clears expired/revoked/consumed encrypted material only; do not erase the evidence or re-open lifecycle state. Idempotent collected retries rely on hash/key, not decrypted secrets.

No permanent offline/cache/downloadable collection credential. Client clears displayed proof on navigation, identity/proof expiry, visibility loss or server expiry; refocus revalidates order and requests a fresh explicit pass if needed. A printed receipt is not collection proof.

## 5. Data, permissions and notices

Use focused new landlord records: `StoreFulfilmentEvent` for readiness/reversal, `StoreCollectionPass` for proof lifecycle, `StoreCollection` for one immutable handover and `StoreFulfilmentNotice` for ready delivery. Snapshot actor ID/name without a deletion-cascading live-user FK. Record original line IDs/quantities/terms hash; do not mutate order price/terms JSON or event registrations. Add immutable record triggers and monotonic proof lifecycle guards, including retention against tenant/order deletion.

Reuse the S1b receipt outbox delivery pattern, queue/claim recovery, bounded retries and safe mail identity, but do not overload a receipt's snapshot or outbox foreign key to send readiness mail. A small separate ready notice references its unique ready event, stores encrypted destination/snapshot, state/attempts/claim/next-retry/sent timestamps; max five delivery attempts, abandoned claims after ten minutes, five-minute retry/sweep, stable Message-ID. Extract shared mechanics only if needed by both real callers, preserving existing receipt behavior; no generic notification platform. Use `StoreOrderReady` in the exhaustive `MailableRenderTest` registry. Mail fakes alone are insufficient.

Add `manage store collection` in PermissionsSeeder, RolePermissions, tenant-safe permissions and Inertia capabilities together. Collection-only staff see Store collections with history-aware navigation, masked buyer email and fulfilment audit, but not buyer financial/capture/refund/payout data or finance actions. Separate controller/DTO rather than exposing the finance-heavy `StoreOrderController` payload. Owner order/finance screens may link to this permission-gated collection view. History remains visible to authorised staff after the switch is paused.

## 6. Native UI and proposed copy

Preserve React/Inertia, square surfaces, teal console accents, native email verification, light/dark and current mobile patterns. No dependency addition. Existing `html5-qrcode` may power a user-started camera, isolated from event check-in. Reuse presentation/camera teardown conventions, **not** event verification endpoints or success handlers. Reuse the existing local QR renderer if available; otherwise obtain approval before adding a QR dependency. Camera denied/unavailable has a fully functional manual route.

**Buyer My orders:** method choice and method-specific progress (Confirmed / Ready / Collected, or Confirmed / Ready / Dispatched / Delivered), original collection instructions and support contact, distinct payment/receipt/email states. Ready collection orders offer “Show collection pass”; dispatched delivery orders offer “Show delivery code” when the recipient is present. Ready copy explicitly says the order remains available; code expiry copy explicitly says the order is unaffected. Expiry and sharing warning stay beside the code. Collected order shows time and original receipt link; no usable pass. Not ready, review, cancelled, refunded and paused states have useful copy and no misleading code.

**Staff Store collections:** preparation/ready/dispatched/completed lists with collection/delivery filters, one selected order, original lines/options/quantities, ready action, native scan/manual entry, matched-order review and “Confirm full handover.” Exact copy: “Check every item before confirming. This records the whole order as collected or delivered using its selected method.” Before confirm, require the check “All items are being handed over.” Show “Already collected” or “Already delivered” without another success sound, an offline warning without optimistic completion, and explicit wrong/expired/replaced/paused/refund-blocked states. Ready email status is separate from fulfilment. Focus errors and restore focus when dialogs close.

Preview uses synthetic Purpledot/order/recipient content and a non-functional illustrative pass. It never scans a real QR, reserves stock, creates a production record, sends mail or takes money. It demonstrates ready, pass, handover, duplicate and refund-blocked states at desktop/mobile widths.
