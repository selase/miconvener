# S1c — Collection and handover design

**Status:** proposal for owner review; no application implementation or activation. Prepared 2026-10-24 against `main` merge `7a90c66c` (PR #50). Native implementation remains the preferred execution method once approved.

## 1. Purpose and current state

Finish the merchandise journey: a seller prepares the full order, the buyer obtains a short-lived collection pass, and authorised staff explicitly confirm physical handover. This works for public merchandise and church, volunteer-group or association stores without requiring dues or Community membership. Private-product eligibility stays in the existing quote flow; later membership lapse does not confiscate a paid order.

S1b is deployed: immutable quotes/lines/terms, original-account payments, stock consumption, receipts/history, full uncollected refunds and payouts. Its `fulfilment_state` already permits `awaiting_preparation`, `ready`, `collected`, `cancelled`, but it exposes no ready/collection mutation. Receipt delivery is already recoverable. Keep those contracts and all venue/event/Community flows.

## 2. Recommended scope and decisions for review

These are Codex recommendations, not previously approved commercial policy:

- **Complete-order collection only.** All purchased lines/quantities are handed over together. No partial handover, delivery, return, exchange, unattended pickup, offline collection, bulk collection or staff override without a valid pass.
- **Pass possession authorises handover.** A pass is a bearer credential issued only to the original verified buyer. We cannot prove physical identity without adding another identity process. Copy: “Anyone you share this pass with can collect your entire order.” Do not collect ID documents/photos or treat matching a phone/email typed by staff as proof. This is an explicit purchaser-authorised bearer handover, not representative membership access.
- **Separate purpose, online only.** Collection pass is not an event ticket, admission QR, receipt number or public order number. Camera scanning and manual entry are alternative inputs to the same server verifier; neither hands goods over automatically.
- **15-minute pass.** One current pass per order; server expiry is authoritative. Original verified email can refresh it, revoking its predecessor. Email/proof expiry prevents new issue and clears the screen; already issued bearer proof remains valid only until its own 15-minute expiry unless revoked/consumed or order/financial eligibility changes. Tell the buyer this boundary; do not pretend an issued screenshot can be recalled from another device.
- **Staff permission separate from money.** New `manage store collection` authorises preparation and handover, not refunds, payout, prices or manual paid status. Recommend built-in Org Superadmin and Org Admin defaults, plus tenant-safe custom roles; ordinary event check-in staff do not inherit it.
- **Ready email is transactional.** One immutable readiness notice per accepted ready transition, with a native order link, original collection/support terms and no pass secret. Delivery failure never changes readiness or prevents a verified buyer reading their pass in My orders. No SMS or marketing messages in this slice.
- **Independent pause switch.** New exact `commerce_collection`, default off. When on, old paid/free orders can be prepared/collected even with catalog/checkout off. When off, no new ready/pass/validation/collection actions; history, receipts, financial recovery and support remain readable/operable. Re-enabling does not renew old pass expiry. Production activation needs a named operator and owner approval.

## 3. Financial and fulfilment state contract

Eligible means `state=confirmed`, `payment_state=paid` with `ledger_state=posted`, or `payment_state=not_required` with zero charged total and `ledger_state=not_applicable`; no `review_reason`, no unresolved/captured-review attempt, pending observation or non-confirmed primary-order refund, and original line allocations all consumed. A confirmed extra-capture refund does not alone block collection once review has legitimately cleared; S1c must not clear financial review itself. No financial-review override is added; existing additional-capture review can remain blocked for finance/support even after its refund completes. Do not promise automatic resumption or silently clear changed-verification evidence.

- `awaiting_preparation → ready`: collection permission, enabled collection switch and eligibility. Preserve original collection/support terms; no price, stock or ledger write. Record actor/time and immutable line/terms identity.
- `ready → awaiting_preparation`: “Return to preparation,” required reason (1–500 characters), same ownership/permission and lock; revoke active passes and record an immutable event. No email is promised for this reversal. A subsequent ready transition has a new event/notice; browser retries do not make another transition/email.
- `ready → collected`: valid unexpired current pass, enabled collection switch, collection permission and current eligibility, explicit staff confirmation that all goods are being handed over. Atomically consume the pass, record one collection and move fulfilment to collected. **No second stock deduction or financial posting**: S1b already consumed stock at confirmed purchase.
- `collected` is terminal for this slice. Preserve immutable actor/name/time/source/pass-ID and line snapshot evidence; no uncollect/delete. Collected-order refunds/returns stay an explicit support policy gap, not an unsafe button.
- Cancellation/refund keeps S1b's finance permission, funded-money checks and confirmed-restock rules. Starting a primary refund immediately blocks collection and invalidates the pass, including `needs_funding` or an unknown provider outcome. Free cancellation does likewise. An extra capture refund must not cancel/restock the valid primary order or revoke a valid pass solely because its separate refund exists; its unresolved financial review still blocks handover.

**One lock order:** Tenant row → Order row → sorted variant advisory/row locks only when the existing refund needs stock. All collection/pass/readiness/refund claim/reversal writers use `CommerceInventoryLocks`; network and mail calls remain outside locks. Collection-first makes the primary refund request fail; refund-first makes collection fail. Durable provider completion is still recorded even if an impossible historical/manual collected state is discovered; show support review, never hide money or restock handed-over goods.

## 4. Proof, idempotency and retention

`StoreCollectionPass` belongs to order/tenant/shop with composite ownership constraints. Store UUID, version, issue/expiry/revocation/consumption timestamps, issuing buyer-email snapshot, SHA-256 verifier hashes and encrypted hidden material sufficient to replay the same successful issue request after a lost response. Do not store a session ID or raw native verification code.

Generate independent 256-bit random QR secret and 12-character random uppercase Crockford Base32 manual code (60 bits; alphabet `0123456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ`); manual input strips spaces/hyphens, uppercases and maps O→0 and I/L→1, then accepts exactly 12 alphabet characters. QR is a purpose-tagged data payload `{purpose:"miconvener.store.collection.v1",pass_id,secret}`; **not a navigable URL**. Manual submission includes the seller's order number/ID, so code lookup is scoped to the authorised seller/order. Reject other QR purposes, oversized input (>2 KiB), invalid UUID/version, unexpected properties and malformed code before lookup. Hash comparisons are constant-time. Secrets never appear in URLs, normal order DTOs, email, logs, Telescope, analytics, browser storage or ticket offline caches.

Issue is POST-only under live original-email proof. A UUID operation key replays the same still-active credential/expiry for the same request; after expiry/revocation, replay gives “pass expired/replaced,” not a new credential. A fresh operation key explicitly rotates; throttle issuance 5 per buyer/order per 10 minutes. At most one unconsumed/unrevoked pass per order; rotation and issue are under the same order lock.

Staff validation is a throttled POST: 5 failed credential attempts per staff+order per 10 minutes, plus 30/minute per staff+tenant and existing authentication/CSRF. Unknown and foreign IDs fail generically and do not disclose products/buyer. Use an HMAC of submitted selectors in limiter keys, not raw secrets. First validate actor/tenant before loading proof. Validation returns only matched seller order, masked buyer email, snapshotted lines, collection terms and a can-confirm result; it does not consume or create a reservation. Confirm resubmits proof + operation key and rechecks everything under locks; a successful scan does not guarantee later confirmation.

Collection has unique order ID and unique order-scoped operation key. A retry by still-authorised staff using the original consumed proof/key returns the same immutable collection even after that proof expires. Reuse with altered proof/order is denied; another operator/request cannot create a second collection. A different request with the same valid consumed pass reports “Already collected” with permitted seller audit; an unrelated wrong credential remains generic. Completed evidence is retained and immutable, including cancelled/expired/revoked pass history; revoke/redact encrypted proof material after it is no longer needed while retaining hashes, IDs and audit. A scheduled purge (daily) clears expired/revoked/consumed encrypted material only; do not erase the evidence or re-open lifecycle state. Idempotent collected retries rely on hash/key, not decrypted secrets.

No permanent offline/cache/downloadable collection credential. Client clears displayed proof on navigation, identity/proof expiry, visibility loss or server expiry; refocus revalidates order and requests a fresh explicit pass if needed. A printed receipt is not collection proof.

## 5. Data, permissions and notices

Use focused new landlord records: `StoreFulfilmentEvent` for readiness/reversal, `StoreCollectionPass` for proof lifecycle, `StoreCollection` for one immutable handover and `StoreFulfilmentNotice` for ready delivery. Snapshot actor ID/name without a deletion-cascading live-user FK. Record original line IDs/quantities/terms hash; do not mutate order price/terms JSON or event registrations. Add immutable record triggers and monotonic proof lifecycle guards, including retention against tenant/order deletion.

Reuse the S1b receipt outbox delivery pattern, queue/claim recovery, bounded retries and safe mail identity, but do not overload a receipt's snapshot or outbox foreign key to send readiness mail. A small separate ready notice references its unique ready event, stores encrypted destination/snapshot, state/attempts/claim/next-retry/sent timestamps; max five delivery attempts, abandoned claims after ten minutes, five-minute retry/sweep, stable Message-ID. Extract shared mechanics only if needed by both real callers, preserving existing receipt behavior; no generic notification platform. Use `StoreOrderReady` in the exhaustive `MailableRenderTest` registry. Mail fakes alone are insufficient.

Add `manage store collection` in PermissionsSeeder, RolePermissions, tenant-safe permissions and Inertia capabilities together. Collection-only staff see Store collections with history-aware navigation, masked buyer email and fulfilment audit, but not buyer financial/capture/refund/payout data or finance actions. Separate controller/DTO rather than exposing the finance-heavy `StoreOrderController` payload. Owner order/finance screens may link to this permission-gated collection view. History remains visible to authorised staff after the switch is paused.

## 6. Native UI and proposed copy

Preserve React/Inertia, square surfaces, teal console accents, native email verification, light/dark and current mobile patterns. No dependency addition. Existing `html5-qrcode` may power a user-started camera, isolated from event check-in. Reuse presentation/camera teardown conventions, **not** event verification endpoints or success handlers. Reuse the existing local QR renderer if available; otherwise obtain approval before adding a QR dependency. Camera denied/unavailable has a fully functional manual route.

**Buyer My orders:** three steps (Confirmed / Ready / Collected), original collection instructions and support contact, distinct payment/receipt/email states. Ready order offers “Show collection pass.” Expiry and sharing warning stay beside the code. Collected order shows time and original receipt link; no usable pass. Not ready, review, cancelled, refunded and paused states have useful copy and no misleading code.

**Staff Store collections:** preparation/ready/collected lists, one selected order, original lines/options/quantities, ready action, native scan/manual entry, matched-order review and “Confirm full handover.” Exact copy: “Check every item before confirming. This records the whole order as collected.” Before confirm, require the check “All items are being handed over.” Show “Already collected” without another success sound, an offline warning without optimistic completion, and explicit wrong/expired/replaced/paused/refund-blocked states. Ready email status is separate from fulfilment. Focus errors and restore focus when dialogs close.

Preview uses synthetic Purpledot/order/recipient content and a non-functional illustrative pass. It never scans a real QR, reserves stock, creates a production record, sends mail or takes money. It demonstrates ready, pass, handover, duplicate and refund-blocked states at desktop/mobile widths.
